FAQ

RepVault — Workout Logging App

Getting Started

What is RepVault?

RepVault is a strength-training workout logger. You organize your training by location and workout, log sets with weight and reps against a 550+ exercise catalog, use built-in rest timers, and review your history and progress over time.

Is RepVault free?

RepVault will be free to start: log a set number of workouts at no cost, then unlock unlimited logging with an optional purchase. No ads, and no account required either way — everything stays on your device. Exact pricing is shown in the App and confirmed by the App Store or Google Play before you’re ever charged.

What platforms does it run on?

RepVault runs on both iPhone and Android. Download it from the Apple App Store or Google Play to get started.

Do I need to create an account?

No. RepVault has no sign-in, no registration, and no account of any kind. Everything is stored locally on your device from the moment you open the App.

Your Data & Privacy

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device, in a local database. RepVault has no server and no cloud backend, so nothing you log ever leaves your phone.

Will my workouts sync across devices?

Not right now — RepVault is local-only by design, so your data lives only on the device you use it on. Cross-device sync may be considered for a future version.

What happens to my data if I uninstall the App or switch phones?

Back it up first and nothing is lost. Profile → Back Up Data writes everything — locations, workouts, exercises, every logged set, your body-weight log and all your photos — to a single file you can save to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email to yourself. Install RepVault on the new phone, tap Restore Data, and pick that file. Without a backup, uninstalling the App or moving phones does permanently erase your workout history. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising SDKs in the App — we never receive a copy of your data because there’s no server to send it to. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

About the App

Can I track workouts at multiple gyms?

Yes. Create a separate location for each gym, home setup, or hotel you train at, and each one keeps its own workouts and exercise order.

Does it include exercise images?

Yes. RepVault ships with a 550+ exercise catalog organized by body part, each with an illustration, and you can add your own custom exercises and photos too.

Can I review my old sets and personal records?

Yes. History keeps every set you’ve logged, merged across every workout an exercise appears in, and Progress surfaces personal records, strength trends, total weight moved, and an anatomical figure showing which muscles you’ve actually been working.

Can I use kilograms instead of pounds?

Yes. Profile → Units switches every weight in the App between pounds and kilograms — what you see and what you type. Nothing you’ve already logged is changed, so you can switch back at any time and your history reads exactly as you entered it.

How do pull-ups and other body-weight exercises get counted?

Tell RepVault what you weigh in Profile → Body Weight and they count properly. On those exercises the weight box is whatever you added — a plate hanging off you — or, on an assisted machine, how much it’s taking off you, so a set reads as “BW + 25”. Not every movement counts as all of you: pull-ups and dips do, a push-up is about two-thirds because your feet carry the rest, and holds like planks aren’t counted at all. Your body weight is kept as a dated list, so add a new entry whenever it changes and older workouts keep the figure you were then.
